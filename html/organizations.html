What Each Organization Does

IFMGA

The International Federation of Mountain Guides Associations — the gold standard for professional mountain guide certification worldwide.

  • Certifies mountain guides across 22 member countries
  • Sets global standards for guide training
  • Ensures consistency in safety and professionalism

UIAA

The International Climbing and Mountaineering Federation — the global authority on climbing equipment safety standards and mountain protection.

  • Sets safety standards for ropes, harnesses, and gear
  • Advocates for mountain access and environment
  • Recognizes competition climbing worldwide

The Alpine Club

Founded in 1857, the world's oldest mountaineering club. Based in London, it continues to be one of the most respected voices in alpinism.

  • Historic leadership in Alpine exploration
  • Publish the Alpine Journal
  • Maintain a historic library and archive

American Alpine Club

The leading climbing organization in the USA — known for its publications, grants, and safety advocacy.

  • Publishes the American Alpine Journal
  • Awards climbing grants and fellowships
  • Advocates for climbing access in the US

Nepal Mountaineering Association

The governing body for all mountaineering activities in Nepal — the gateway to the world's highest peaks.

  • Issues climbing permits for 26 peaks (below 7000m)
  • Trains local climbing Sherpa guides
  • Manages waste and environmental stewardship

Club Alpin Français

France's national mountaineering club, established in 1874. The CAF operates hundreds of mountain huts across the French Alps.

  • Operates over 120 mountain refuges in France
  • Trains volunteers and young climbers
  • Promotes mountain culture and conservation
